Reading notes, week of the 24th

Kept coming back to the same idea this week, that most of what we call a platform is a filing cabinet somebody put a login on. The interesting question is not how to build a better cabinet. It is what happens when the document itself is the program, and the cabinet is just a folder.

The counterargument is real: a folder full of clever HTML files is a maintenance burden that lands entirely on one person. But that person is me, and I would rather owe maintenance to myself than rent to a company that will sunset the product in four years.

Undo is the most underrated feature in software and the first one cut. Files that outlive the tool that made them are the whole point, and everything I wrote in a hosted editor in 2009 is gone while everything I wrote in a text file is not.
